In an emergency

Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies, fire or crime in progress.
Strong shaking near the coast? Once the shaking stops, walk inland and uphill right away. Don't wait for an official tsunami alert, because a local tsunami can arrive in minutes.
Told to evacuate? Go. Take the go-bag, medications and pets, and follow the routes officials give, which may differ from the usual ones below.

Official alerts & status

SMC Alert : San Mateo County's emergency texts, calls and emails. Sign up every phone in the household.
Genasys Protect : look up your evacuation zone by address and see live zone status during incidents. Write your zone name down.
MyShake (California Earthquake Early Warning) : seconds of warning before shaking arrives. Install the app and turn on phone emergency alerts.
NWS San Francisco Bay Area : warnings, surf and storm forecasts.
San Mateo County Dept. of Emergency Management : county emergency updates and preparedness guides.
City of Pacifica : city notices and closures.

Utilities

North Coast County Water District : water notices for Pacifica.
AirNow, Pacifica : air quality and smoke.

Routes out of Pacifica

North: Hwy 1 to Daly City, then I-280 or Skyline Blvd (SR-35).
East: Sharp Park Rd up to Skyline Blvd (SR-35) and I-280.
South: Hwy 1 through the Tom Lantos Tunnels to Half Moon Bay, then Hwy 92 east over the hill.
Pacifica has few ways out, and slides, fire or a quake can close any of them. Check before you go: Caltrans QuickMap · CHP incidents · Tsunami hazard maps · Open Red Cross shelters
